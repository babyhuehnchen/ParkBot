# ParkBot 1.2.3

## What's new

- Picked contacts now appear in a neat box with their name above their phone number.
- Tap the × in the top corner to clear the contact and return to number entry.
- Your selected contact is remembered when you reopen ParkBot. Your timing, message, and SIM stay unchanged when you clear it.

## Install

Download **ParkBot-1.2.3.apk** and open it on an Android 8.0 or newer phone. It updates an existing release installation when signed with the same release key.

Always check your parking service's confirmation. SMS and parking charges may apply.
