# Changelog

## 1.2.1

- Added regular Dark mode and ordered modes as Light, Dark, Follow System Settings.
- Converted OLED Dark and Material 3 into separate switches.
- Changed standard palettes to neutral white and dark grey.
- Made Material 3 take priority over OLED, including background colors.
- Preserved existing theme choices when upgrading.

## 1.2.0

- Hid the start-time box when Start now is selected and centered the stop-time box.
- Removed duplicate SIM selection from Setup.
- Added saved theme selection.

## 1.1.3

- Fixed content overlapping the status bar, including after starting/stopping a session.

## 1.1.2

- Calculated renewal times from the last sent minute, ignoring seconds.

## 1.1.1

- Aligned scheduled SMS times to whole minutes.

## 1.1.0

- Adopted Material 3 controls.
- Made the disabled start-time control more obvious.
- Moved SIM selection onto the main screen.
- Allowed intervals starting at one minute.

## 1.0.0

- Initial native Android parking SMS scheduler.
