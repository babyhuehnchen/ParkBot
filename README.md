# 🅿️ ParkBot

**Parking, on repeat.**

ParkBot sends your parking SMS automatically at the times you choose. Pick a number, write your message, and decide when to start, how often to send, and when to stop.

**[Download ParkBot](https://github.com/babyhuehnchen/ParkBot/releases)** · [What's new](CHANGELOG.md)

For Android 8.0 and newer.

## Get started

1. **Install ParkBot.** Download the APK from the releases page and open it on your phone.
2. **Set your times.** Choose **Start now** or a start time, then choose when to stop.
3. **Choose an interval.** Enter how many minutes to wait between messages. Include any waiting period required by your parking service.
4. **Choose the recipient and message.** Pick a contact or enter the parking number, select your SIM, and type the exact text your parking service needs.
5. **Tap Start parking.** Allow the requested permissions and review the details before confirming.

With **Start now**, the first message is scheduled for the next full minute. ParkBot stops automatically at your stop time, or you can tap **Stop parking** at any point.

## See what's happening

The main screen shows your next SMS, your last sent message time, and how many messages have been sent. Open **History** to check previous sends or see why sending stopped.

Picked contacts appear in a box with their name and number. Tap **×** to clear the contact and enter a number or choose someone else. Your other choices stay as they are.

Your choices are remembered for next time.

## Make it yours

Open **Setup → Theme** and choose **Light**, **Dark**, or **Follow System Settings**.

- **OLED Dark** gives dark mode a pure-black background.
- **Material 3** uses colors that match your phone wallpaper where supported. When enabled, it controls the background too, so OLED Dark is temporarily inactive.

Tap **Apply** to save your choice.

## A few things to know

**Always check your parking service's confirmation.** Sending an SMS does not guarantee that your parking ticket was accepted. Normal parking fees and SMS charges still apply.

Your phone needs mobile service to send messages. Battery-saving settings can delay them; if this happens, open **Setup** and check the app's battery settings. Keep your phone switched on while ParkBot is running.

ParkBot keeps your settings and history on your phone. It does not read your incoming messages or use the internet.
